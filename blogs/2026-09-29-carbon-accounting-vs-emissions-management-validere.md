In the same week, the corporate sustainability team asks for the company's GHG inventory, while an environmental lead at a compressor station still has to turn March fuel for one engine into an emissions calculation the facility can review and defend. Both requests get called carbon. They are different jobs, and oil and gas operators usually need both.
